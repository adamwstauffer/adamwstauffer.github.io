Adam W. Stauffer

Finance and economics lecturer. Former ETF trader and CIO. Builds courses, and other things, with AI.

I teach finance and economics at the Shidler College of Business, University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, to undergraduate, MBA and executive MBA students in Honolulu, Vietnam and online. Before teaching, I was a trader and market-maker in U.S.-listed ETFs at Barclays Capital and Lehman Brothers, founded and ran Springline Capital as Chief Investment Officer, and launched Advent Software's global solution for hedge funds and fund administrators. I hold an MBA in Finance from Wharton and was a CFA charterholder from 2004 to 2011.

Every course project I teach is built around one problem: AI now drafts the first pass that junior analysts used to build, and that first pass was where they learned judgment.
